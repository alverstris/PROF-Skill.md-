For an error comparison subtract each estimate from the supplied reference and take the magnitude. For sine, evaluate $`-\sin x`$ at the basepoint.

Square 0.02 first. Apply $`1+\epsilon/2`$ and $`1+\epsilon/2+3\epsilon^2/8`$ to the ratio, then multiply each by 1000 seconds. The error requirement is an absolute time, so compare time differences rather than just dimensionless corrections. Section F identifies a physical effect omitted by the model.

To assess the universal claim, recall what happens when the second derivative vanishes and what a local error statement actually guarantees.
