Hint P2

Rewrite the reciprocal as $`(1+u)^{-1}`$ with $`u=-2x`$. Keep its constant and linear terms, and do the same for the exponential with input $`3x`$. In the product, a quadratic term can come from three sources: a square in either factor or two linear terms. Have you included all three sources, or only one?

Hint P3

The displacement is $`h=0.04`$, not 2.04. For the logarithm, $`f'(x)=1/x`$ and $`f''(x)=-1/x^2`$. Evaluate these at 2. In the quadratic formula the coefficient of the square is half the second derivative at the base point. Subtract each estimate from the supplied comparison value, then take magnitudes.
