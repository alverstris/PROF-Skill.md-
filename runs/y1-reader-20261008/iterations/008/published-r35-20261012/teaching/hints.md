Hint D008-E3

Let $`h=x-2`$. Keep $`f(2)`$ symbolically, and evaluate $`f'(2)`$ and $`f''(2)`$ before constructing the polynomial in $`h`$. Subtracting $`\ln2`$ at the end cancels the constant, so it does not need a decimal evaluation. Differentiating $`Ch^2`$ twice with respect to $`x`$ gives $`2C`$ because $`dh/dx=1`$.

Hint D008-E4

Rewrite the reciprocal square root as $`(1+2x)^{-1/2}`$. Its power-formula input is $`2x`$, so square the whole input for its quadratic contribution. For the product's quadratic coefficient, collect constant times quadratic, linear times linear, and quadratic times constant. A zero linear coefficient does not force a zero quadratic coefficient.
