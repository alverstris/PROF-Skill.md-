Q1. For f(x)=ln(x)/x restricted to the closed interval [1,sqrt(e)], find the maximum and minimum values and every input where each is attained. Target: distinguish an optimizing input from the function value and respect a changed domain. Criterion: correct attained values/inputs and a domain-based argument.

Q2. A cylinder now has both a bottom and a lid. Its fixed volume is V>0; ignore material thickness and seams and use a uniform surface-area cost. Find the positive radius and height that minimize its total surface area, the ratio h/r, and the minimum area. Construct the new surface-area formula, reduce to one variable using the volume constraint, and justify a global minimum on the whole feasible domain. Target: reconstruct a model when a controlling geometric condition changes. Criterion: correct added lid term, domain, stationary calculation, global warrant and interpreted dimensions/value.

Q3. Revisit after a break if useful; the timing is an adjustable study suggestion. First, with the main explanation covered, state the categories of points/boundary behavior that must be considered when seeking absolute extrema, and explain why solving f'=0 alone is insufficient. Then consider a wire of total length 1, bent into two squares. Each piece must now have length at least 1/4. Determine the permitted interval for cut position x, construct total area from the two perimeters, and find its maximum and minimum values and all attaining cuts. Finally remove the minimum-length bound but require both pieces to be strictly positive: which of these extrema is still attained, and what happens to the greatest possible area? Justify your conclusions. Target: retrieval of the search principle and transfer to changed constraints and existence of an extremum. Criterion: complete feasible domains, candidate comparison, attained results and a correct distinction between a limiting bound and an achieved maximum.
