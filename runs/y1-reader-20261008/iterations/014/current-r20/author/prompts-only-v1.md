D014 prompts-only v1 for independent presolution checking

P1. For y=x², take base x=3 and dx=-0.1. Find dy, the tangent estimate of the new y, and the exact Δy. State which equality is exact and why the two changes differ. Criterion: preserve signs and distinguish a differential from the actual change.

P2. Estimate the cube root of63.7 using base64. Write both the differential construction and the corresponding small-parameter binomial expression; show that the two estimates agree. Identify the step that introduces approximation. Criterion: map an absolute input change to a dimensionless relative one.

P3. A function H is0 for x<0 and5 for x>0; it is not defined at0. Its derivative is0 everywhere in its domain. Does this contradict the claim that a function with zero derivative on an interval is constant? Explain by identifying the domain condition. Criterion: say why a single constant need not span two disconnected intervals.

P4. Find an antiderivative F of x²(x³+2)^5 with F(0)=0. Then assess the separate proposal that -e^(-x²)/2 is an antiderivative of e^(-x²). Differentiate the proposal and say exactly what integrand it does match. Criterion: choose/adjust the inner derivative and use a condition without treating every exponential as the same pattern.

P5. Find F on0<x<1 such that F'(x)=1/[x(ln x)²] and F(e^(-1))=0. State the domain of your formula and verify its derivative. Criterion: the changed power of ln x must determine the primitive, and a negative ln x must not cause an unjustified rejection.

P6. Write the function with derivative sin x cos x and value0 at x=π/2 in each form A(x)=sin²x/2+C_A and B(x)=-cos²x/2+C_B. Determine both constants, show the expressions agree, and explain why their constants differ. Criterion: distinguish the same family from identical constants in two representations.

P7. Later, with the explanations covered: (a) for y=ln x estimate y at x=1.04 from x=1 and state why your result is approximate; (b) find a function J with J'(x)=cos(3x),J(0)=2 and check it exactly; (c) a classmate claims ln(ln x) is an antiderivative of1/(x ln x) on every x>0. Give the real intervals on which the integrand is defined, repair the formula and explain the role of absolute value. Criterion: discriminate finite-change approximation from exact recovery of a function and retain domain conditions.
