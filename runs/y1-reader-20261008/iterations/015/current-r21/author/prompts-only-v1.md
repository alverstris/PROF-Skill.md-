D015 Lecture 16: frozen prompts, revision 1

All tasks are author-generated applications of the assigned lecture, not historical exam questions. Prior teaching planned: derivative/ODE distinction, operator action L[u]=u'+xu, Gaussian solution, separation and excluded zeros, implicit versus explicit forms and inverse branches, geometric slope condition, perpendicular slopes, ellipse family and domain conditions. Baseline supplies algebra, derivatives, antiderivatives, logarithms, inverse functions and elementary separation.

Q1. For L[u]=u'+xu, write L[x²] explicitly. Then solve L[y]=0 with y(0)=-2, and separately with y(0)=0. Check each solution in the original equation and explain why division by y requires special care in the second case.
Criterion: distinguish the input function from the output function; satisfy both the equation and initial condition; recover any excluded solution.

Q2. Solve y'=x(1+y²), y(0)=0, on an interval containing zero. Identify f(x), g(y), h(y), H(y) and F(x) in the separation template. Give the implicit relation first, then select an inverse branch and the largest open interval containing zero on which your explicit solution remains finite. Explain why no constant solution was discarded.
Criterion: match each function to its role, preserve the inverse range, and check the original equation and domain.

Q3. A curve through (2,-4) has tangent slope twice the slope of the ray from the origin at every point where x is nonzero. Construct and solve its differential equation on x>0. At (2,-4), find the ray and tangent slopes. Explain what the equation does and does not say at x=0.
Criterion: obtain the equation from geometry rather than only recognise a parabola; use the initial point; distinguish extending a formula from satisfying an equation at a forbidden input.

Q4. Find the curve through (2,1) that meets the family y=ax² perpendicularly. Give its implicit equation, horizontal and vertical semiaxis lengths, and its explicit branch through (2,1). State where that branch satisfies y'=-x/(2y). Identify the member of the parabola family through (2,1), and check the two slopes there. Explain the status of the full closed ellipse and its points on the x-axis.
Criterion: connect family slope to perpendicular slope, integrate correctly, select branch/domain from the point, and distinguish a geometric curve from a single differentiable graph.

Q5. Later, without first reopening the worked examples, solve (i) y'=3x², y(1)=2 and (ii) y'=2y/x, y(-1)=2 on x<0. For each, choose a method and state what feature of the equation justifies that choice. Check both answers by differentiation and initial data. A proposed solution of (ii) is y=2x: identify the first condition it fails. Finally state the nonzero conditions that must be checked before separating y'=f(x)g(y).
Criterion: discriminate direct integration from separation, use a negative-x interval consistently, and distinguish a correct initial value from a correct differential equation.
