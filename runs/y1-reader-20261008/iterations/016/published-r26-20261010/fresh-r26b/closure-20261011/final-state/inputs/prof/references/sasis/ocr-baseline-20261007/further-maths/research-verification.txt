Boundary decisions and source issues

1. Matrices may have complex entries. Row-by-column multiplication, transpose, inverses and transformations do not automatically grant the Additional Pure eigenvalue/diagonalisation theory or a quantum conjugate inner product.
2. The selected specification supplies vector-product components but explicitly excludes its separate magnitude formula from required knowledge in 4.04g. Component calculation and an ordinary norm are still legitimate. This exclusion does not prohibit deriving a new identity from permitted premises.
3. The current 6.04e stage-two cell both mentions hinged bodies and explicitly excludes them. The original printed page was rendered and visually inspected; this is not an extraction-column accident. The packet does not guess an assessment convention. Any hinge task must state its mechanical model; an unresolved course convention requires a focused student question.
4. Pearson coding shorthand must be qualified: negative scaling of one variable reverses correlation sign. The packet supplies sign(ab) rather than repeating an unqualified claim that every linear coding leaves r unchanged.
5. Exact OCR rank-table statistics are distinguished from raw signed/rank sums used in a normal approximation. Critical values cannot be applied to a differently defined statistic. Independent review led to explicit folded statistics, tail-direction checks, sampling/symmetry conditions and continuity corrections.
6. Independent review added the booklet's hemisphere-shell, circular-arc and pyramid centres, the fourth inverse integration pattern and the ln(1+x) endpoint x=1. It also corrected speed versus signed tangential velocity, signed power, smooth-impact tangential components and the solid/washer and lamina conditions, and explained why zero constraint force alone need not terminate a limiting circular path. Pyramid location is stated as one-quarter of the centroid-to-vertex vector, distinguishing perpendicular height from slanted distance. Rod symmetry and the semicircle specialization are distinguished from separately printed booklet formulas.
7. The selected Mechanics option supplies a=v dv/dx even though Pure Core excludes it as an automatic calculus fact. Qualification-specific exclusions do not erase rules actually supplied by another selected component.
8. Probability-weighted averages, exponential mathematics and complex numbers are established. Physical probability laws, quantum measurement, partition functions and advanced field calculus require actual new premises in the university notes. The student's ability to calculate is distinguished from authority to select a physical model.
