<a id="h1"></a>

Hint P1. First mark the five endpoints of four equal pieces of $`[0,2]`$. For a left-endpoint sum, use the first four marks for the heights and leave the last mark unused. Each chosen height still needs multiplication by the common width. On one piece, compare its chosen height with the curve heights to its right.

<a id="h2"></a>

Hint P2. Write the right sum with square indices $`1,\ldots,n`$ and the left sum with square indices $`0,\ldots,n-1`$. Their common factor is $`b^3/n^3`$. Align the two lists before subtracting: only their unmatched endpoints remain. Then use that $`x^2`$ is increasing on $`[0,b]`$ to place each sampled rectangle between its left and right counterparts.

Hint P4. Start from the principal in one short interval, $`12000t\,\Delta t`$ dollars. That portion has $`1-t`$ years to accrue simple interest. Multiply the portion by its own final-debt factor before adding. For the uniform comparison, replace the borrowing rate while keeping the same time-dependent factor.

<a id="h5"></a>

Hint P5. At a sample time $`c_i`$, the contribution is the signed rate there times the duration. Locate where the rate changes sign. For the amount moving either way, treat the magnitude of the outward contribution as positive; for net change retain its negative sign. The initial 10 litres and the four-minute duration enter different requested quantities.
