Hints for Definite integrals

Use the hint for the step you need, then return to your attempt. Complete answers are on the separate [solutions page](solutions.md#d016-solutions-start).

<a id="d016-hints-p1"></a>

Hint P1

The width is $`(2-0)/4`$. The first right sample is one width to the right of 0; continue by adding the same width. Evaluate $`3-x`$ at those coordinates before multiplying the sum of heights by the width. Since this line slopes down, compare the right height with the heights to its left inside the same strip. The exact region is a trapezium with vertical parallel sides of lengths $`g(0)`$ and $`g(2)`$.

Hint P2

The width is $`2/n`$, but the right sample is $`1+2i/n`$ because the interval starts at 1. Square this whole expression. You will have a constant term, a term proportional to $`i`$, and a term proportional to $`i^2`$ to sum. When subtracting right and left sums, write their height lists: every interior boundary height appears in both lists and cancels.

Hint P3

At height $`z`$, similar triangles make the proposed side equal to its base side times its remaining-height fraction. The last stack layer still has side 1 just below height $`n`$, while the proposed pyramid approaches its apex there. Test a height strictly inside that last layer. For a working replacement, revisit why the main construction places the outer apex one full layer above the stack.

Hint P4

Evaluate an antiderivative at the variable upper bound and at 0. The graph crosses the axis where $`2x-1=0`$; use this point to split the geometric area into two nonnegative pieces. Average value divides the signed integral by the whole interval length. To locate decrease of $`A`$, use the sign of its derivative rather than the growing interval length alone.

Hint P5

A contribution sampled at $`c`$ has principal $`(6000+6000c)\Delta t`$ and remaining age $`1-c`$ years. Apply simple interest to that small principal before summing. For the interest-only integral, factor out 6000 and use $`(1+t)(1-t)=1-t^2`$. Principal and interest must both finish with dollar units.

Hint P6

Split Plan V at $`t=1/2`$. First compare principal by adding rate times duration. For each debt contribution use its remaining age $`1-t`$; a single common full-year age will erase the distinction between the plans. To examine one changed rate value, compare finite sums term by term: only strips whose sample equals that exact point can change. Bound their total change before taking the limit.
