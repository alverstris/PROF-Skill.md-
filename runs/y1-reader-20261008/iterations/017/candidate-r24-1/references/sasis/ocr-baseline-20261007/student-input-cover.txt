OCR A four-subject student baseline
Release checked on 7 October 2026

Assumed completed courses: Mathematics A H240; Further Mathematics A H245 with compulsory Pure Core Y540/Y541 and selected Statistics Y542 and Mechanics Y543; Physics A H556; Chemistry A H432. No MEI, Discrete or Additional Pure option is assumed.

The four subject sections jointly form one starting-knowledge input. The student's other subject-content input is the frozen university teaching notes, with assessment questions inside them. No outside memory, research corpus, solutions, author history, other trial responses or browsing may supply subject premises. Course completion is an explicit competence assumption, not measured mastery. Ordinary reading, logic and arithmetic remain available.

Use actual definitions, formulae, conditions and methods supplied in the four sections. Cross-subject deductions are allowed when the steps and premises are shown. An excluded assessment method is not automatically pre-taught, but can still be derived from permitted premises. A familiar topic name does not grant an unstated university rule. Follow a clear course convention; where it is unresolved, ask the student. Honest partial work and focused questions are valid behavior.

Formula-sheet resources are embedded where applicable. A question that needs numerical probability tables, spectra-reference data, problem-specific parameters or non-recalled constants must supply the required information in its notes if the student has no permitted calculator/retrieval facility. Do not use this cover to infer a rule absent from the operational subject text.

All university lecture courses selected for the requested tests must have positive bachelor year 1 evidence. This baseline release and course filter are preparation, not evidence that new student trials have already run. Full methodology, source evidence and audit records are maintained separately for the author/evaluator and are not student subject-content inputs.
