[CHR-05] Scope decisions and safeguards against over/under-granting

Coverage was checked against the actual H432 learning outcomes, not a generic A-level chemistry list. Quantitative treatment is preserved for rate laws/units, half-life, Arrhenius, K_c/K_p, acid constants/buffers, lattice/solution cycles, entropy/Gibbs and cell potentials. The baseline spells out formulas, symbol meanings, applicable conditions, mole balances and graph interpretations.

The specification explicitly excludes or does not require various details: integrated rate forms, quadratic solving in chemistry equilibrium/weak-acid assessments, hybridisation, detailed origins of transition-ion colour, aromatic-proton splitting analysis and halogen-organic mass-spectrum interpretation. There is no named K_sp learning outcome in H432; importing Salters solubility-product material would enlarge the declared chemistry syllabus. Application of general equilibrium principles to a new supplied constant remains possible.

“Not pre-taught” is not a ban on derivation. With a Mathematics/Further Mathematics baseline, a learner may legitimately derive and solve a quadratic from stated stoichiometry and K_a, even though H432 itself does not require that algebraic calculation. Likewise ordinary algebra can generate the two-temperature Arrhenius relation and logarithmic buffer rearrangement from their supplied parent equations. The baseline marks the need for additional chemical premises, rather than preventing sound use of existing premises and mathematics. No Additional Pure or Discrete Mathematics knowledge was imported; the separate Further Mathematics route is Statistics and Mechanics as specified by the user.

There is no permission to assume university statistical thermodynamics simply from the word entropy. The baseline supplies qualitative energy dispersal and quantitative tabulated entropy/Gibbs methods, while leaving microstate formulae, partition functions and chemical potentials to new notes. Related distinctions are made for activity-based equilibrium, Nernst, advanced organic mechanisms and more advanced spectroscopy.

Content explanations are original descriptions of standard scientific facts and methods, organised for the declared student model. Source prose, question text, screenshots and tables are not republished in the baseline. Downloaded official reference PDFs and verification images remain separately identifiable in `reference/` for auditing. Synced `sources/` files were not edited.
