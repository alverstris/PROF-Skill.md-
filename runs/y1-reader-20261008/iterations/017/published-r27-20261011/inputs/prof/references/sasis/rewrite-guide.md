# SASIS: diagnose a failure and repair PROF

Revision: 2026-10-10-r24. Apply only during explicitly requested PROF self-iteration.

Repair PROF, never the frozen generated document. Each action below describes the behavior to require in PROF and verify in a new complete generation. It is not permission to patch the evaluated notes. Publish the revised skill to GitHub main before that fresh generation.

SASIS is the dedicated reader. Diagnose what the document makes available at each point, not how well a simulated student performs. Read the full original report and exact frozen document/baseline. Preserve them. A question, an unsupported conclusion or an uncertain interpretation is evidence to investigate, not an automatic teaching verdict.

## Establish the route and causes

For each substantive concern, locate the claim/representation, available baseline or earlier premises, required connection and conclusion. Check legitimate alternative routes. An actually available short deduction may close a suspected gap; an expert's ability to invent extensive untaught machinery does not establish reader accessibility. Later passages cannot retroactively supply an earlier required premise.

Distinguish introduced definitions/laws/assumptions from deductions. New scientific premises need clear status, meaning and conditions and independent technical checking; they need not be derived from school knowledge. A technically correct premise may still be poorly introduced. A coherent deduction from a false premise is still scientifically inadequate.

Collect all supported issues across the entire document, required source portions, reader report and independent checks. Keep multiple causes and manifestations where evidenced. The first unsupported connection orders repair, never ends the audit. Mark dependent reasoning conditional/blocked and revisit it after repair; inspect independent later material now. Do not convert uncertainty into invented defects.

## Choose the concrete repair

| Evidenced cause | Behavior to encode in PROF | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name-only, circular or unclear meaning | Replace the introduction with meaning in available terms, purpose, conditions and a minimal useful instance before reliance. | Fresh reader locates the meaning and reconstructs its first consequential use. |
| Missing representation mapping | Identify relevant objects, grouping, indices/axes, units, domain and correspondence between words, equations and figures at the transition. Show how the representation expresses the intended relation. | Reader reconstructs the mapped relation and affected later interpretations from supplied passages. |
| Missing prerequisite connection | Inspect full baseline first. If operation exists there, supply its connection to this use. If genuinely new, teach the smallest complete necessary bridge or reorder the route. | Baseline locators and fresh reading show the connection is available before reliance, without redundant course reteaching. |
| Unsupported inference, cancellation, limit or approximation | State the premises and conditions; expose the pivotal intermediate connection and why the operation is allowed. Keep routine established algebra concise. | Reader reconstructs the step; independent reviewer checks validity and relevant domain/boundary cases. |
| Undefined notation or changing symbol role | Define/map at first use, distinguish fixed from varying quantities and reconcile notation at the seam. | Reader can identify each role and the subsequent conclusion without guessing a convention. |
| New law/model presented as a deduction | Identify it as an introduced empirical/model premise, explain its meaning and scope, and verify it externally. | Reader distinguishes premise from consequence; technical evidence checks the premise's accuracy and conditions. |
| Method choice or connected example obscured | Make the decision criterion and connecting reasoning visible in the worked route; integrate disconnected fragments. | Fresh reading recovers why the method applies and how premises lead to the interpreted result. |
| Ambiguity yields different conclusions | Rewrite the ambiguous referent/condition at its first consequential use. Preserve legitimate alternatives explicitly where relevant. | Reader's permitted interpretations no longer conceal incompatible conclusions; reviewer verifies intended scope. |
| Unsettled course convention | Read discriminating course evidence; if genuinely unresolved, ask the actual user a focused defining question and hold dependent acceptance. | Actual supplied decision and consistency check, or an honestly pending dependency. |
| Scientific/source/extraction error | Independently verify the original source and correct the false premise plus all affected explanations, examples and help. Record source correction. | Independent technical derivation/source check and fresh reader report for the corrected route. |
| Exercise/help unsupported at its position | Put essential teaching before the demand; repair matched hints/solutions and navigation. Later answers cannot serve as earlier prerequisites. | Reader inspects prompt and help in order and reconstructs each supported demand and explanation. No added task battery is required. |
| Cross-section or cross-lecture missing dependency | Include the actual required prior teaching in this document and reconcile notation/conditions at the seam. | Fresh reader uses that supplied passage; previous-reader memory is unnecessary. |
| Irrelevant expansion or redundant baseline reteaching obscures the route | Remove the evidenced obstruction while retaining necessary connections. Keep local novelty even for an expert baseline. | Fresh reading of the shortened route and affected successes shows support remains available. |
| Baseline or input missing/truncated | Repair delivery or verify/freeze a genuine baseline correction separately. Do not edit the lesson to hide the input fault. | New admission record and fresh reader with both complete artifacts; original result retained as an input/profile issue. |
| Reader/reviewer error despite available route | Point to the real prior passage and valid deduction; preserve the original concern and evidence-backed resolution. | Independent inspection establishes the route. Do not invent a skill rule or extra explanation merely to avoid a reported concern. |
| Unauthorised subject access or unsupported remembered rule | Invalidate affected evidence; repair the boundary/delivery and dispatch a fresh reader. | Actual access record and reconstruction from the two permitted inputs, with honest isolation limits. |

## Extent, skill change and closure

Match the scope of the PROF change to the demonstrated cause. Strengthen the relevant operative instruction or execution trigger for a local failure; revise the dependency/authoring procedure for a structural failure. Preserve the evaluated document unchanged and regenerate the full teaching in a fresh context. Recheck affected passages, help and seams in that new output. Missing necessary explanations outweigh modest useful extra detail; neither maximum brevity nor maximum explicitness is the objective.

For each issue retain ID, exact original evidence and locators, supported causes, affected dependencies, chosen repair, before/after passages, relevant source checks, any PROF edit and fresh verification. Unresolved questions have a concrete next action. Do not close with a checkbox, heading, page count or fluent final answer.

Every independently verified material failure requiring correction warrants a PROF repair in this run. If an existing rule was ignored, repair its activation/execution requirement; if absent or inadequate, repair the rule. For source, baseline, reader or runner failures, encode the required handling and recovery rather than a false subject-matter correction. Specify the demonstrated trigger, concrete action and observable evidence. A disproved concern is not a verified failure. Do not embed this lecture's answer in general instructions.

Freeze and validate actual changed skill files; push the complete revised PROF to GitHub main and verify actual published bytes. Then start a new author with no inherited conversation, the fetched published skill, original task/source conditions and complete baseline. Retain only operational goals, constraints, identities, queue and outstanding tasks; exclude old documents, diagnoses, reviews and proposed answers from generation inputs. Open a separate fresh SASIS reader on the whole new document; independently check correctness, affected successes and new issues. Preserve old evidence outside this new context. No erasure of live context or pretraining may be claimed without an actual capability.

Publication of revised PROF happens before regeneration and does not imply acceptance. Close the lecture only after the full audit and material issues are resolved in new generation under that published version. Publish and verify closure evidence, reload actual main, and proceed with fresh contexts to the next material. Later changes reopen affected earlier checks. Preserve corpus state, not author or reader memory. A first successful document does not complete the assigned corpus.
