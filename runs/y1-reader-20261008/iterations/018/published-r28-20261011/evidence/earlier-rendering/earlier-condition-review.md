## Trigger and scope

C1 is the D018 statement that an accumulation input below its base is permitted when the integrand is defined on the intervening interval. Definedness alone does not establish existence of the finite definite integral. This review asks whether earlier accepted text makes the same substitution: having operand values or a writable formula is treated as sufficient for a derivative, inverse, integral, limit or other result to exist.

C3 is the D018 claim that changing a function at one point changes no rectangle-sum area because its contribution has zero width. A sampled finite rectangle can change; the limiting integral is unchanged. Root and coordinator regard the literal finite-sum warrant as a narrow object/limit-status defect. My original D018 concern disposition, which recorded the accessible valid limiting interpretation, remains unchanged in its original file. This additive dependency audit checks the narrow literal finite-object versus limiting-result family without requiring new exercises or a wholesale audit.
