## Presentation audit

**Pages 1–10:** Outline and section breaks were read. The capacitor paradox on p4 must compare two surfaces sharing one oriented boundary; the schematic is not a quantitative construction of magnetic field everywhere. Old Ampere law without displacement current is inadequate for the changing capacitor. Pages5–6 correctly motivate Id=ε0 dΦE/dt and the Ampere-Maxwell law; equality with wire current presumes the full ideal plate flux and consistent signs. It is not actual conduction through the vacuum gap. Maxwell's addition and the no-monopole equation are introduced laws; the plate relation is a deduction with geometry assumptions. Page9's source language is a summary of coupled equations, not a temporally sequential causal mechanism. Page10's four integral equations and separate Lorentz force q(E+v×B) have correct signs and constants for vacuum/total sources. Closed surfaces, contour orientation and fixed-loop Faraday assumptions matter; a moving loop additionally involves motional emf.

**Pages 11–20:** All radiation/static frames were inspected, including p13's sparse black-background image. The linked animations were not executed; no time sequence is claimed from a single frame. The traveling-pulse graph on p15 shows x=vt for fixed phase of f(x−vt). Pages 16–17 deliberately use f(x+vt), propagation −x for positive v; p18 switches to sin(kx−ωt), propagation +x. This is a real convention change with explicit signs, not a contradiction. Wavelength, k=2π/λ, ω=2πf, T=1/f and v=ω/k=λf recompute correctly. In p16 “velocity v” while moving left means speed v and signed x-velocity −v. Graph amplitudes are distances from midline, not peak-to-peak values.

Recovered p19's full electromagnetic spectrum was read: each labeled wavelength/frequency pair obeys λf≈3×10⁸m/s (e.g. 3 m at 10⁸ Hz; 4×10⁻⁷ m at 7.5×10¹⁴ Hz). Band names overlap and visible color boundaries are approximate. “60 Hz ac current” is a frequency marker, not proof that a household wire is an efficient radiating antenna. Cellular labels represent the historical schematic, not current service bands. Page20's field-arrow sizes represent signed components/directions at each x; E and B at the same point are in phase. Its “out of phase oscillators” refers to different spatial positions and should not be reused to claim a phase lag between E and B. Equal drawn heights do not mean equal SI magnitudes or common units.

**Pages 21–26:** The PRS/group/in-class title slides are pointers, not full problem statements. No unidentified “Problem 1” or separate class worksheet was manufactured. Page23's c, E/B and transversality statements apply to a single traveling vacuum plane wave. They do not apply indiscriminately to standing fields, near fields or material waves; at common nodes the ratio is undefined although E=cB remains valid in magnitude. Page24's complete direction table was independently checked: x×y=z, y×z=x, z×x=y; reversing each product gives −z,−x,−y. All six listed coordinate phases p̂·r agree. Fixed polarization unit vectors are orientations of positive components; instantaneous arrows can reverse with the sinusoid.

**Pages 27–33:** The cylinder energy derivation on p28 correctly sums both field energies, sets dz=c dt, uses E=cB and ε0μ0c²=1, and obtains S=EB/μ0. The same scalar reduction requires a traveling plane wave. Page29 correctly defines S=E×B/μ0, dimensions J/(m²s)=W/m² and sinusoidal intensity I=E0B0/(2μ0)=E0²/(2μ0c)=cB0²/(2μ0). The factor 1/2 averages a squared sinusoid with peak amplitudes. Page30's steady resistive cylinder has axial E and azimuthal B, hence inward S; integrating gives I²R, as independently checked in the shared chapter review. Pages32–33 reverse circulating E while B remains upward, correctly reversing radial S. “Increasing/decreasing current” must refer to the shown positive current or magnitude: universally dUB/dt=L I I′, not merely a sign test on I′. These are ideal field illustrations, not apparatus wiring diagrams or experimental outcomes.
