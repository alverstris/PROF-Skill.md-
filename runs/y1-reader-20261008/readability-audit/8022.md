## Source caveats

The official index supplies one PDF per session. In-class demo references and incidental references to other course material were not silently expanded into additional required assets. Published content is distinguished from unavailable original classroom material.

- **D126:** PDF page5 has a blank upper slide position; printed slide numbering jumps from8 to10. All published content is present in the downloaded PDF. Adjacent material is course orientation followed by mathematics review; no unresolved substantive reference to the blank slot was found. A missing number alone is not an accessibility failure.
- **D126:** PDF page2 (printed slides3–4) is raster-only, with zero extracted text. It was visually inspected at 3x render scale: main title, course URL http://web.mit.edu/8.022/www/, staff names and meeting table are recoverable. Tiny browser screenshot body text is intrinsically blurred, but is an illustrative homepage preview, not a physics claim, assigned exercise, or needed course-logistics instruction. No external homepage was used to supply missing lecture content. This nonconsequential screenshot detail does not block faithful reading of the assigned lecture.
- **D133:** PDF page1 contains the title slide only; subsequent printed slide numbering begins at3. The next page contains quiz comments and a previous-lecture recap, then the EMF/circuit sequence. No unresolved substantive reference to absent printed slide2 was found; all published content is readable.
- **D141:** Printed slide15 refers to a complex-number handout and future recitation. The indexed required lecture asset is the PDF, which includes the needed complex-number definition, complex-plane figure, Euler identity and phasor representation. A separate handout was not added to the assigned source packet.
- **D142:** PDF has14 file pages while printed handout numbering runs1,3–15 and slide numbering begins1,4–30. The title lists exam discussion, followed by recap and a continuous circuit sequence. No unresolved substantive reference to absent printed slides2–3 was found. Eligibility concerns all published indexed lecture content, not unpublished class material.
- **D147:** This PDF has one slide per page (unlike earlier two-up files), and page20 includes substantial speaker notes below the slide; these notes were included in visual review.
