## Blocking finding

**S01/D105:** Chapter 1 section 1.2.1, PDF page 6 (printed page 5), Figure 1.2.4: several numeric contour-level labels merge into dense raster linework and remain unreliably legible in the native page and 5x detail render. The preceding prose explicitly identifies the labels as the values of the plotted levels, but the caption/prose does not give the individual levels. Equation 1.2.3, axes and qualitative contour geometry remain readable; the missing quantitative annotations were not guessed or reconstructed.

The defect lies within S01’s assigned §§1.1–1.6. S02 assigns only §1.6 (PDF pp14–15), so it remains eligible independently. No companion asset was missing.

## Source caveats and association decisions

- **S26 presentation p17:** A hollow-square label identifies a generic single circuit element and repeats as its voltage subscript. The title and immediately adjacent pp18–20 instantiate the same diagram as R/V_R, C/V_C and L/V_L; p21 compares those cases. Its role is explicitly recoverable within the packet. The native p17 and adjoining pages are retained in the visual receipts.
- **S25 presentation p22:** A trailing hollow-square artifact follows R. The R/(2L) fraction, grouping parentheses and exponent remain legible; no required mathematical component is lost.
- **S05, S28 and S33 PRS:** Isolated trailing square artifacts are documented with native views. The intended field quantities, equation terms or path difference are explicit in the same pages/packet.
- **S07/S08, Chapter5 pp6 and15:** Miniature simulation UI controls/values are soft. The scientific scenes and physical roles are explicitly stated in the legible captions and adjacent prose. No blurry UI value was inferred or used to pass a numerical claim.
- **S24:** The official index associates `prs_w10d1.pdf` (30 pages) with this studio. That exact file was inspected and retained despite the filename.
- Historical links and applet/demo screenshots were inspected as published PDF content; external interactive functionality is outside this packet audit.
